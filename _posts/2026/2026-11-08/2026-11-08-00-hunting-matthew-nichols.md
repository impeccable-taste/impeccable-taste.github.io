<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Tara's brother, the titular Matthew Nichols, was obsessed with ***The Blair Witch Project*** and trying to film a similar movie with his friend Jordan. Then they disappeared, flash forward to the present day and we're watching a documentary about how Tara's trying to investigate what happened, and all that.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yup!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![hunting-matthew-nichols](hunting-matthew-nichols.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Eeeeeh. I chose this one on the Infinite Pile of Movies To Watch because someone online said it reminded them of ***Lake Mungo***, one of the subtlest, most terrifying movies I've ever watched. Well, uh, this ain't it, that's for sure. There's *almost* something great hiding in this movie, there are times where it is very self aware (kind of how [***Butterfly Kisses***]({% post_url 2025/2025-07-15/2025-07-15-00-butterfly-kisses %}) plays with the genre conventions), there are some fun "hah, true crime, am I rite?" moments, but... yeah. Not my kind of crap, I guess. It's even worse when I see some people really loved the last uh 10 minutes or so (that's usually a bad sign on a found footage movie, but what do I know), and to me those were some of the least subtle, least interesting moments in recent history. Of course, and I know I'm being very unfair, I'm comparing this to two of the best in the genre, so...<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I'd have a bit more of footage of The Good Times. We barely see anything the two (apparently) amateur super film buffs are trying to film!

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> The production created a fictional online presence for the town of Port Rupert, British Columbia, maintaining social media accounts for years to immerse audiences in the story world.

Something tells me this won't be one of those things people keep bringing up twentysomething years after the fact, unlike the whole ***Blair Witch*** thing...
