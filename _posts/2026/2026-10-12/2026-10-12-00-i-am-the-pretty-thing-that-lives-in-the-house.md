<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Lily is a live-in nurse for an elderly horror writer, Iris, who keeps thinking the poor nurse is none other than the main character from her first best seller. That must get tiring very, very quickly but I mean, I guess it goes with the territory. Anyway, the thing is that they live in this big house in the middle of nowhere and loneliness is kind of getting to poor Lily, who is starting to read Iris' books and think that, maybe, they were not fiction after all.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> barely but yes<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> they toy with it so much that it made me a bit annoyed, even!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![i-am-the-pretty-thing-that-lives-in-the-house](i-am-the-pretty-thing-that-lives-in-the-house.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Okay, let's get this out of the way: good slow burn movies have a payoff that make things worth the wait, and if we're talking about horror they should also slowly build tension. This, well, is not that. Unfortunately the ending feels a bit like "oh, well, *I guess*" because once again this is a neat idea for a half an hour short. I mean it would have been incredible! But as it is, almostninety minutes is a bit too much for the little story it has to tell.<br/>*That said*, I thought it was really beautifully shot, it felt really creepy a couple of times or two and I have zero complaints about the cast. I just wish there was a bit more here!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I'd be happy if they read a bit more of the *other* novels Iris wrote, to be honest.

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> The narrative of the story says the young bride was brought to the house her husband built for her in 1812, but the dress and hairstyle she is wearing, as a ghost, is from the period of the 1850s to the 1860s. Since she was murdered soon after moving into the house, the dress and hairstyle do not match her backstory.

Look, that outfit was really gorgeous so only the worst kind of nerd would be bothered by this.
