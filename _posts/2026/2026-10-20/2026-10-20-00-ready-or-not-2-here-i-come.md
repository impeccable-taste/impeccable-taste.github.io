<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Samara Weaving survived that devilish hide-and-seek game on her wedding night, but as it turns out, she still needed to face an even greater challenge...<br/>*RECORD SCRATCH*<br/>... reuniting with her estranged sister??? Whaaaaaat!?<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Nope.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> take a wild guess!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![ready-or-not-2-here-i-come](ready-or-not-2-here-i-come.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> The first ***Ready or Not*** was a really dumb but fun movie that somehow avoided the biggest trap where it is literally a movie about a deadly game of hide and seek so they could pretty well have written an absolutely passive protagonist-victim, and, well, that wasn't the case because Samara Weaving played her as resourceful and actually making the best possible decisions. That continues here *except* for some family drama that, thankfully, is not too dragged out because it is pretty fun to see her and Kathryn Newton interact, they've got some real chemistry together. And every single scene with Elijah Wood is delightful, I don't know. All in all, a surprisingly fun movie!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I wish there was a lot more Sarah Michelle Gellar. That's it, that's what I'd do to improve this film.

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> Samara Weaving's Grace gets stabbed through the body with a large long metal spike; a serious injury. Grace pulls out the spike but doesn't bleed and is never seen in pain or impaired by the injury later on and it's never mentioned again.

She also had glass and stuff on her arm just about an hour later, and who could forget that she had been shot through the hand and stabbed twice just the day before, and she also shrugged that off. You know, like it tends to happen in horror movies, specially horror comedies...?
