<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Setting subtlety aside, and since there are already a few movies that went "what if ***M3GAN*** but you FUCKED her?", the people behind that series decide to go for it themselves. Under the pretense of talking about, you know, like, people who have a chatgpt gf (ew) and become kind of dependent on that and so on and so forth. But yeah killer robot, except this time she's aaaall grown up!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> About ten minutes more.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Yes they do! They even poke fun at how dumb it is to throw an eight there so hey, full marks.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![soulm8te](soulm8te.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah... while not as openly dumb and misogynistic as [***Subservience***]({% post_url 2026/2026-03-08/2026-03-08-00-subservience %}), [***Companion***]({% post_url 2025/2025-02-25/2025-02-25-00-companion %}) is still the only good one of these. Mind you, this has some funny scenes, the leading ladybot is good at Moving Like A Robot and a pretty gory and well deserved kill, but the movie itself is uh, not amazing. Too bad because I *do* like the two ***M3GANs*** and I expected this one to be dumbly funny too.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> ***M3GAN*** had a kill count that almost got to two digits, [***M3GAN 2.0***]({% post_url 2025/2025-10-31/2025-10-31-00-m3gan-2.0 %}) surprisingly got like twentysomething kills, this movie gets less than five. I kinda expected better from the franchise!
