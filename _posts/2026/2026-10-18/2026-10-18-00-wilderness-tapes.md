<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> A dude who calls himself "Prospector Pete" trespasses to go check out this abandoned gold mine, and of course to film everything for his youtube channel. [serious narrator voice] What follows is the unedited footage that was found on the site. Also boo.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yup!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope, even though it's got kind of a twilightzonesque intro/outro segments, the narrator doesn't say "wilderness tapes". He says both "wilderness" and "tapes", but, you know.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![wilderness-tapes](wilderness-tapes.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Look, at some point if you find out about an abandoned old mine in which like 75 people died, and those people were (and you know this beforehand, mind you) immigrants forced to work in bad conditions, and *very clearly* it was the mine owner's fault that they died so he could get the insurance money... Well, if you decide to *go there* and on top of that you *go there at night, alone*, maybe (and I do not mean to victim blame) it is your fault you're megahaunted. Anyway, fun stuff although our guy might be one of the dumbest people on earth. Oh, wait, he's a youtuber, of course he is dumb.<br/>Also, for some reason found it very funny that he did that little ad spot for a torchlight for his channel, then proceeded to not use the light even when running out of batteries.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> Missed opportunity to go for the unexpected ***Leprechaun*** crossover and have our friend Warwick going "I WANT ME GOLD" and all that...

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>

No trivia listed, so I will say that the actual title is ***Wilderness Tapes: Volume One***, which is amazing because it clearly says "yup, there'll be more of these no matter if you like them or not!"
