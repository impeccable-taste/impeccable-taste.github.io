<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> So back in 193X there was this confusing couple of days where too many people died at this house, all in really weird circumstances. Now, in 201X, your job is to solve the mystery with the help of this cool-but-what-the-fuck machine that can play scenes back. Kind of a psychophony machine a certain sherlock holmes would have loved to have? Another entry in the whole "static investigation" subgenre of video games!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> about 9 hours in my case, remember i'm dumb as hell.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Not exactly, no. Almost, but no.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![the-incident-at-galley-house](the-incident-at-galley-house.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah, uh, this was... excellent. I played a couple of hours after buying it, but then i binged the rest of the game in a single sitting the next day. What a fun ride this was, and what a pleasure it was to slowly (i'm dumb) realize what was going on, and what it meant for the case. Very cool stuff, so fun i actually, for once, got all the cheevios like a tryhard.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I'd add a couple more of weird things to do with the machine that were *there* from the start but you didn't realize you could do. Yeah, that should be simple to add.
