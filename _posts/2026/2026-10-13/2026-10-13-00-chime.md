<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Matsuoka is a chef that would love to be called an artist, but even though he's trying to find a job at a restaurant he is kind of unhireable except as a cooking teacher. One of his pupils is this really awkward, off putting guy who keeps talking about how he can hear this chime in the distance and it is filling him with... dread, or something like that. Then as you might guess Matsuoka finds a great job and the movie ends with a happy, quirky musical number!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Half that, actually<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Yes!

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Okay, no, I didn't spoil the end so if you were about to watch it for the ending - well, the ending is definitely worth it but it is not exactly "people dancing happily on the streets to the main theme of the movie".<br/>This is a movie by Kiyoshi Kurosawa, the guy who directed both ***Cure*** (no relation to Robert Smith) and the amazingly spooky ***Kairo***... and yeah, it's the same kind of deliciously dreadful slow burn where things get progressively worse and more tense. Just the way we like it, friends.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> i want more of those extremely awkward home scenes with the family members that don't even look at each other, brrrr.
