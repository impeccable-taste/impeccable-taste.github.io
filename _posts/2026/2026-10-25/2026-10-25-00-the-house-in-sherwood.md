<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Angela is a scammer/medium who is going to spend some days investigating this allegedly haunted house. Meanwhile, Karen is this crazy lady who has been stalking Angela for a while after she lost a ton of money because she trusted her investment choices on a medium. Which, I mean, that's on you, friend. So on one hand we've got the investigation, which consists on Angela basically live streaming everything she does in her life and overanalizing every single little noise while being completely blind to the obvious stuff happening two steps away from her, and on the other hand we've got a crazy as hell person that is trying to "prove" Angela's a phony by making things look haunted AND sending her poison pen letters. Lovely. Oh, also she is recording all of her stalking/crimes and basically for some reason narrating everything she does *even when she's trying to be quiet and stealthy* which, uh, that's a choice I guess.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> So, uh, yeah. Let's be honest: this was not good. It DID have a good premise that might have been fun for an even shorter movie ("someone is stalking/faking the stuff a scammer/medium is pretending to notice, without knowing that the place is actually megahaunted"), but... Too long for what it is, completely bonkers in a non-relatable way when it comes to Karen talking to the camera as if she was a streamer going "okay chat", and some really baffling editing choices. There's a very fun short short there, the problem is that it's buried under 70 minutes of runtime.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I actually am going to say that I liked the extremely obvious "hidden ghosts" in this because they were hilariously unsubtle, so I would have loved more of that, please.
