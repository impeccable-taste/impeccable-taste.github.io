<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Hey, it's that dude spending some days ~~away from his wife and newborn baby~~ investigating that haunted house! Not the one in the title, the other one and uh, yah.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yes. I'll say one thing: the guy knows that this wouldn't work if it was a bit too long, so the movies in this series always are a bit over an hour and that's that.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Haha, no.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![the-blackwell-ghost](the-blackwell-ghost-5.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Surprisingly for this genre (and series), this movie actually gets going from basically the beginning, instead of making us wait for who knows how long to get to the fireworks factory. Usually, on bad found footage movies, that means "the last couple of minutes". And... the story progresses! Which, again, what a surprise! If anything the problem this time is that, well, it only progresses *that much* before the movie starts to basically prepare things for the next one. But, hey, I mean.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> There is a hilarious moment on this one when you realize that when he finally decides to investigate what's the source of all that banging and knocking, *it is literally the first time he's got any close while the noise is happening* in three movies. My friend, you spent several movies looking at a corridor, don't tell me at no point you weren't like "maybe I should take a couple of steps that way"?
