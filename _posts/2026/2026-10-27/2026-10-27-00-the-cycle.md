<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Deborah Ann Woll is a forensic expert on this one, really great on her field, who sometimes helps the police with some investigations. Case in point, she gets called to help with this very weird crime scene where her estranged dad (who she hasn't seen in like thirtysomething years) has turned up. As a really out of place corpse, ruh roh!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah, this was excellent. My only complaint is that i didn't really love the visual design/direction of a specific part of the movie, but the rest was really good. Very slow burn at first where it feels like it's *almost* sure going to be "just" a thriller (even though there's been some really gory imagery by the beginning of the movie), then hah, the movie points at you and goes "well, I bet you didn't see *this* coming!". Which... I actually did, but, and this is key: I didn't mind. It is great to go blind into a movie and be suprised, but your movie shouldn't depend on a twist to be good, as frequent readers of this, your favourite blog, know. And this was really solid and at times really chilling.<br/>
