One that involves extreme competivity, leeches, no kisses and uh, a lot of surprisingly graphical scenes. Oh, and tennis.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> It is exactly that!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope...

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![bloody-tennis](suspiria.png)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Oh boy, this was yet another blind watch and I wasn't expecting something this bizarre, brutal and blatantly "I really, *really* liked Suspiria, okay?"-ish. Very cool movie with some harrowing character development, disgusting stuff and ewww, way too many leeches!<br/>
