<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> The new weird girl in town finds a weird aztec whisle in her weird high school locker (the locker is weird, the high school is okay i guess?). In detention she and her bullies that suddenly become friends discover that it has an inscription that says something like "summon the dead", and of course from that point on people can't stop whistling. With unsexy results!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Nope and I can't even understand why not<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Yeah, at least there's that!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![whistle](whistle.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> I don't know why but this one really disappointed me. The titular whistle's design is amazing, but then the rest of the movie is a attempt to ape the ***Final Destination*** kind of deal, except without any sense of humour. It somehow manages to be both a sometimes surprisingly gory movie and at the same time the lamest teen garbage you've ever seen. Too bad.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> Just... drop the young-pastor-who-is-a-drug-dealer-and-evil thing from the script completely. It felt so tacked in, so cartoonishly dumb (in the ***Cartoon All-Stars to the Rescue*** style, really)... By far the worst part of the movie.
