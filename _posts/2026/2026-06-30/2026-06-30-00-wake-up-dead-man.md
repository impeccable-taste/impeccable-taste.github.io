<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> In a really catholic kind of move, a young priest is sent "far away" to another church after doing something wrong. The plot twist happens very early in the movie because "something wrong" involved the very relatable need to punch a catholic deacon, and not something involving kids as you'd expect from that institution, but I digress.<br/>You know how awkward it is to move somewhere new: there's already an old priest there, the congregation is as unwelcoming as possible, there's also a murder and you gotta get used to the new bakery. Yeah, it's another Benoit Blanc movie!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Hahaha no, not even close.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![wake-up-dead-man](wake-up-dead-man.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah, ***Glass Onion*** was a bit of a letdown even though it was a very funny movie, this time we're dealing with something a lot more along the lines of the original ***Knives Out*** although way more grounded in setting. Which actually works really well! It knows it needs to be funny, it also knows when to take the story seriously and Mr Blanc is still as delightful as always. Surprising what you can get when you let good actors talk to each other with a compelling story, huh.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I like that this time around the little (or sometimes not as little) breadcrumbs and clues were enough to let me solve the case on my own, which made me feel very smort and also like, maybe, Benoit Blanc and I could be friends. So, the way to improve it would be to cast me as Benoit's dumb friend who keeps getting kidnapped, etc.
