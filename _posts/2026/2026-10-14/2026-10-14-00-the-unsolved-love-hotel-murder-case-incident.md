{% include endorsement.html %}<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Well yeah, this was fun. It definitely knows what it's going for, it lasts exactly as long as it should and it gets genuinely creepy here and there. There are some very fun moments by the end and the characters are surprisingly normal for a horror movie. As in, they act exactly like regular people would in that situation. Fun!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> there's this blink-and-you-miss-it hidden ghost and I'm sure there were more but I only saw the one. I want more of that AND of the time shenanigans.
