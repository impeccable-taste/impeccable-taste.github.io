<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> We have a family that is grieving (the commitment in this case depends to the specific family member) the loss of their son, who died while killing brown people in Afghanistan. We also have the titular Guest, a certain David Collins, who says he was the son's best friend while they were committing war crimes, and also that he wants to say hi and all that. The family offers him a place to stay and oh, things get considerably bloody soon because it turns out this guy's a bit nutty!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Nah.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> This is a surprisingly fun thriller with some horrific elements! I'm sure David was more sympathetic to a US audience what with all the arr troops and all that, but it was fun enough as a setup and things take really little to get bonkers with our very special Captain America. Maika Monroe is, as usual, really great, our boy David is scarily confident and suave, I dunno. A fun time was had by all. Very very in the style of the Master of Horror, Mr John Carpenter himself. I bet he found this one very funny too!<br/>
