<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> So a couple of years ago I watched [***The Blackwell Ghost***]({% post_url 2024/2024-10-01/2024-10-01-00-the-blackwell-ghost %}), which was a, uh, not that amazing found footage movie. The thing is that back then I saw there were like a million parts after the first one. Not a huge feat considering nothing really happened on the first one, but anyway. What better excuse than [spooktober]({% link tags/spooktober/index.html %}) to watch more of this?<br/>Oh, right, blablabla, guy goes to this haunted house blabla he only has three days to film because the previous owner is selling the place, blablabla, you know the drill. Ah, and there's a record the ghost keeps playing.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yes. Actually, not only it's shorter, it fake-ends like 10 minutes before the actual movie ending (then they RECOVER SOME EXTRA FOOTAGE OH DEAR and it's still under 90 minutes, take that!)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> ... no, but the funny thing is they DO say ***The Blackwell Ghost*** several times when talking about the first movie so I'm going to say "yes, they do" because it's not like rules have ever mattered.

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Okay, I *know* I'm going to be in the minority here but I actually found this one entertaining. Like, the guy must have seen that people loved [***Bad Ben***]({% post_url 2024/2024-10-25/2024-10-25-00-bad-ben %}) and he must have thought "well I can also play up the inherent goofiness in the premise". And... it works?<br/>This time around our dude doesn't notice anything that is happening literally a metre away from him unless he's looking directly into it, and sometimes not even then. In-world, he's read the first movie reviews, so now he's talking to the camera all the time like a twitch streamer would do (and hilariously he's adding notes to some of the most annoying IMDb goofs in the universe, so more power to him).<br/>The movie itself is not great but you know, it has a certain crappy charm. You could even add a little sad music moment where I admit that even though it's a crappy movie about a serial killer grandma, in the end I kind of consider her a frie-<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> dude, how the hell do you have some *actually creepy* moment with this buried box thing and then proceed to just never bring it up again? I suppose I'll find out when I watch ***The Blackwell Ghost 3***...
