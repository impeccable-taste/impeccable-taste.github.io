<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Macaulay Macaulay Culkin Culkin's younger brother (no, not Kieran, Rory) plays EURONYMOUS, the original leader of MAYHEM in this (very heavily) fictionalized account of how Trve Norwegian Black Metal started back in the nineties! Yeah, you know, the most anime of all metal music genres? That!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Almost two hours<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Yes! And it is the lamest "we're the ***Lords of Chaos***!" title drop, which made me really happy.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![lords-of-chaos](lords-of-chaos.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Look, I am very aware of how this one was a bit controversial on release, what with people with zero sense of humour feeling really angry when the movie didn't take itself as seriously as they take their favourite music genre. I don't care what those losers think, this movie manages to nail the balancing act where the tone is exactly what it needs to be: this is a story about a bunch of extremely depressed, sad, stupid and violent teenagers that get way way more than they were asking for. At times it is almost scary in how dreadful things feel, often it is hilarious. The casting is just perfect, and also hey they burned some churches and I was 100% hootering and hollering then. Also, our favourite racist sociopath and murderer Louis Cachet *hated* this movie, so of course that's another point for it.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> my only wish is that Hellhammer got more screentime because damn, that's a chill cool late eighties metalhead that's all about party and booze!
