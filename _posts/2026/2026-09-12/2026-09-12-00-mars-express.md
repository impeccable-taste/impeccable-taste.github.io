<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Two students are missing! It's a job for detective extraordinaire Aline Ruby and her partner, half-robot-half-hologram-all-robocop Carlos "Carl·litos" Rivera! Will they uncover a way-too-big conspiracy in this cyberpunk sci-fi story? Oh you bet!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yes!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> No. It's on screen diegetically twice, but, yah.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![mars-express](mars-express.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah, this was a very fun thriller. It's been a while since I last watched anything even remotely cyberpunk so it was fun to get back to one of those movies where they kinda need to both say "this is normal for us" but at the same time "here, this is how things work here, in this movie's reality". A fun enough mystery, my friend guessed the outcome (i didn't, i'm dumb) and we had a lot of fun. Good!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> More Carlos. He was clearly the MVP.
