<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Okay, so the ***V/H/S*** marathon continues and this time it's 198X, or more specifically 1985! This time around we have a bunch of kids swimming at the lake, an earthquake in Mexico, some avant garde theatre, a sort-of-a-quinceañera, the world's greatest Siouxsie fan and the frame narrative is about this weird mushroom thing. Kinda like the ***Super Mario Bros*** movie from 1993.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> no, *but* hear me out: i didn't even notice this time.<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nah. Well I mean, they keep mentioning the year, does that count?

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> I... am kind of speechless. True fans of this, your favourite blog, know that if there's a guilty pleasure genre we like it is [found footage]({% link tags/found-footage/index.html %}), if there's a format we love here it is anthologies (because in the end that means "it's a series of short films/stories and if this one doesn't work the next one will"), and the next song is about shooting blood out of y-<br/>But, seriously, this is the sixth movie in the series, this is the point where usually most horror franchises go to hell (not laudatory), and yet... I don't think there was a single "weak short" on this one. They're all bangers, Brent. Extremely recommended!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I *kinda* want to know a bit more about both the "kids at the lake" and "weird family celebrating something" ones. That and really, Netflix should do a short series about the metroymedio detective and his plucky goth assistant.
