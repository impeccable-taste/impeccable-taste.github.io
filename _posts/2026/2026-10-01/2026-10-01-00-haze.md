<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Picture this: you're a japanese man (most likely a japanese salaryman but that's not really explored in the movie). You wake up and you barely remember anything at all, also you're just wearing your underwear or something. Also you're trapped in this extremely narrow concrete tunnel. Also it's kinda pitch black and there doesn't seem to be any escape. Mondays, am I rite?<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yeah, less than 50 minutes actually!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![haze](haze.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> So this is one of Tsukamoto's movies (you'll definitely know what's the deal here when I say "he also directed the ***Tetsuo*** movies) and uh it's an experience alright. Extremely claustrophobic and anxiety inducing for the first half, it loses a bit on the second part... I think the (not too blatant) gore is a lot less effective than "guy biting on a rusted metal pipe and you hear how those teef are being ground to dust or something? dunno, it's just some really disgusting (laudatory) sound design!". Very disorientating, also one of those "huh?" kind of movies once it's over, but yah. I mean we like [***Begotten***]({% post_url 2025/2025-04-01/2025-04-01-00-begotten %}) here so take this with a grain of salt.<br/>
