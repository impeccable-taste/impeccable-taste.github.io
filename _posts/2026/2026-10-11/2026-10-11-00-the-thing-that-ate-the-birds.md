<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Something's eating Abel's birds, which is not great news because he's also dealing with a barely functioning marriage right now... thankfully he's got a big rifle and that'll solve everything!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> It's a short, it's less than 15 minutes long!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nah...

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![the-thing-that-ate-the-birds](the-thing-that-ate-the-birds.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Pretty fun little short, very obviously one of those where the dude sitting by your side whispers "[well you see, the gun here is actually not a gun, it's...]({% link tags/it-s-a-metaphor-d-uh/index.html %})" and all that, which in this case feels just right. Lovely northern accents, very cool effects and you know, it definitely didn't overstay its welcome. Nice. Plus, that title!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> If anything my problem was the usual youtube thing where the thumbnail kind of spoils the ending, what the fuck. What would be next, ***The Planet of the Apes*** featuring the statue of liberty on its cover or something?

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> The writer/director couple explain their motivation in D&CFilms: "The Thing That Ate The Birds was born from our frustration with society. At the time of writing, BREXIT was at the forefront of the media. We were acutely alarmed at the divisive nature of the debate and the blame and criticism of 'the other.' It felt like everyone was losing their heads. As parents too, we were, and still are fearful of the kind of society our children are going to grow into. ... The marriage between Gamekeeping, our fear for society and our love of monsters really created this cautionary tale."

Somehow this is marked as spoiler although I am very very puzzled about how this would ruin the experience in any way.
