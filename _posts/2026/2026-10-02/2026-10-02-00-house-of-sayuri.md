<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Meet the Kamiki family! They're moving to their new house! Of course, this being japan and a horror movie, the place's haunted by (you guessed it) the titular Sayuri. Tragedy will befall the family, but before long let's say that this movie goes... uh... places that you wouldn't expect.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> About twenty minutes over that<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> They do say ***"Sayuri"***, the original title.

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Another one by Koji Shiraishi and of course we need to ask ourselves if this is like the wonderful ***Noroi***, the cool [***Ura Horror***]({% post_url 2025/2025-06-03/2025-06-03-00-ura-horror %}) and [***About a Place in the Kinki Region***]({% post_url 2026/2026-06-17/2026-06-17-00-kinki %}), the extremely flawed [***A Record of Sweet Murder***]({% post_url 2026/2026-01-23/2026-01-23-00-a-record-of-sweet-murder %}) and of course how could we forget the terrible [***Shirome***]({% post_url 2025/2025-04-14/2025-04-14-00-shirome %}). I know, I know, we always do the same kind of recap but trust me, this dude is really a land of contrasts and so on.<br/>In this case... I think we'll have to sigh and point towards "flawed, with very fun moments that are almost enough to redeem it but I mean, what was that about". The tonal whiplash on this one is impressive, it's like we have three different movies that somehow are fighting to appear on screen. The problem is that they don't really work, *but* I'd be lying if I said I didn't laugh with this...<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I honestly didn't like the whole MEGAFAT bit that much. Not like you can't have fat ghosts, I mean, you can always remember Slimer and ***Casper***'s uncle Fatso... but yeah.

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> Social Commentary: Beneath the scares, the film touches on the Japanese phenomenon of hikikomori (social recluses), as the titular ghost was a recluse before her death.

I mean, sure, but I... "social commentary" might be doing a lot of heavy lifting there...
