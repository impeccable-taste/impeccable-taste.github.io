<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> For some reason Amy is convinced that she'll die tomorrow. This is something she brings up in conversations and on top of being a bummer, it is kind of infectious because people start to feel the same dread she's feeling. But anyway, Amy shows she's not a loser and delivers on her promise. More or less.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yup!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> No, but uhm, it's because Amy doesn't speak about herself in the third person, she definitely says "I die tomorrow" and "I am dying tomorrow" and all that a million times.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![she-dies-tomorrow](she-dies-tomorrow.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> A few years back I was renting this flat that was actually a duplex. This sounds very impressive except it wasn't, it was a tiny place with mold and the rain would leak and whatever. The important bit for this anecdote is that it had some stairs and they were some of the most unstable stairs in the universe, to the point where when I moved there I kept joking about how one day I would be trying to run downstairs to pee (the bathroom was downstairs, my bed upstairs), and haha wouldn't that be a ridiculous death. Then, one morning, I was really hangover and kinda dragging myself downstairs, I tripped and for a few seconds I was, like, enjoying weightlessness, the sweet certainty of "i'm going to break my neck here or at least hurt my back super hard". I thought about my dog briefly because she's the most important thing in the universe and *then* when the realization that I was about to die hit, I remember distinctly thinking "well, that was disappointing."<br/>The plot twist is not that this blog is haunted (you see, I didn't die) but I was thinking about that today. Did I like the movie? Well it sure was entertaining and it kept me guessing. Would I recommend it to anyone? Nope. I guess you had to be there.<br/><br/>Falling down the stairs.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> [Don Draperilish] I know what you're thinking: this is a gloomy motion picture... but, what if... she *laughs* tomorrow? Huh, huh? Any takers? Aw, forget about it. [smokes, cheats on his wife]
