<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Paul buys a way too ornate mirror on a whim, then basically immediately after has a car accident. Barbara, the fiancee and now widow thinks she's seeing Paul in the reflection of that mirror when she's alone with it. I mean, not just seeing it, they can even interact and smooch and stuff like that. Oh, there's also a psychiatrist and cults involved. I mean, everybody's invited to the party, it looks like?<br/><br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> A tiny bit over that<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nope.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![fear-no-evil](fear-no-evil.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> This was a blind watch and surprisingly fun, actually, more even when you realize it was a made-for-tv thing. Obviously it is dated as hell and cheesy as only something filmed before the moon landing can be (there are a few scenes showing computers that are hilariously crappy), but that doesn't mean it wasn't enjoyable. The script keeps adding very little touches of things that make you go "hmmm I wonder", which *did* make it feel like an investigation. I don't know, I had fun.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> at some point they should have said something like "look in the mirror! it's horrible!" and shown just black on screen so you'd see your own reflection and feel very owned by a movie made almost 50 years ago.

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> Initial original titles for this TV film were ***The Devil*** and ***Dead of Night***.

Ah, see, they *did* say THE DEVIL several times, yes.
