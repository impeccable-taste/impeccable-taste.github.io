<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> So this lady, Asa, is executed along with her boyfriend because apparently she's a witch and a satan lover and stuff like that, which let me tell you, is not a good way to make me like the "good" characters on your movie, but anyway. With her last breath, she curses Zoidberg and also her brother's bloodline. Now, two hundred years later, this clumsy dude will accidentally bring her back to life...!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> yup!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> yeah, if you're watching the italian dub at least. Not sure about ***Black Sunday*** or any of the other alternative titles.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![la-maschera-del-demonio](la-maschera-del-demonio.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Extremely striking film, extremely gorgeous shots, super cool movie all along. Sure, sure, it's 1960 so of course as usual a lot of things are dated, I also watched the movie in Italian (which I guess means I chose the *dubbed* version somehow), but I don't know. A really beautiful, scary movie that feels way more modern than a lot of the cinema from that era that I've watched. Huge recommend, this one.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> if anything, Barbara Steele's double act is great *but* she's way, way better as the Very Evil Asa as opposed to the Too Goody-good Katia. I wish we had more of the former!

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> Mario Bava was a big fan of Nikolay Gogol's short story, ***Viy***, on which this film's plot and characters are based. He recounted that he often used to read the story to his children and that the tale scared them so much they insisted on sleeping in bed with their father. He so admired the horror elements of ***Viy*** that when given the chance to choose the material for a film he was to direct, he immediately selected it in order to make this film.

Well, that certainly explains a lot of stuff!
