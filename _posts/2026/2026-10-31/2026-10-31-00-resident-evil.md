Just like with ***Barbarian*** and [***Weapons***]({% post_url 2025/2025-09-13/2025-09-13-00-weapons %}), Mr Cregger knows how to balance horror with the obvious reactions and the movie is way, way better for it. Anyway, yeah, this is a seriously fun, good movie.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I like that I, as a person who hasn't really played any of the games in the series seriously, could get the references *but* at no point they got in the way of the movie. Like, we didn't have someone pausing, looking at the camera and going "I better grab these green leaves............". So uh, more of that, please.
