<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Turns out there's a huge conspiracy to keep the existence of aliens a secret, and knowing this is a Señor Spielbergo joint the question is: is this going to go for the spooky route (***War of the Worlds***), the sense-of-wonder option (***E.T.*** and ***Close Encounters***) or the we-do-not-talk-about-that-one one (***Indy 4***)?<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Nah, it's basically two hours and a half long<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Hell yeah, they do!

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Yeah, I had a great time with this one. Josh O'Connor does a really good job, for sure, but Emily Blunt definitely steals the show. It's Señor "Sense-of-Wonder" Spielbergo so thankfully this time you get a lot of people looking in awe at something just slightly off camera, a really positive outlook on things and some optimistic opinions about empathy. Yeah yeah sure sure we all saw how things went with covid so this might be *too* optimistic, but who cares, I had lots of fun!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> geeze, those cgi animals look fake as hell. I bet it couldn't have been much more expensive to just bring a couple of real raccoons or whatever instead.

<span class="reviewsection">Trivia about the IMDb trivia:</span>
> To prepare for her alien voice performance, Emily Blunt deliberately rejected AI (artificial intelligence) vocal modifications. She instead went into a sound recording booth to manually perform her character's alien clicking and guttural babbling sounds. Blunt said she was "terrified of AI," and was determined to deliver a strictly human vocal performance.

And I'm glad she did, because it *does* sound really freaky in a good way!
