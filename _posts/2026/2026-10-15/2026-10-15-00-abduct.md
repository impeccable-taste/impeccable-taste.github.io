<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> Emma and Chris go to this little getaway with a couple of their friends, to spend the night there and have some nice time. There's this weird green light in the distance, though, and when he goes investigate it, Chris seems to disappear. The next day, a random naked dude arrives, he says he *is* Chris and he was abducted. So a regular Tuesday, of course.<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Yu-yup!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> They almost, almost do because they say "abducted" a lot, but they don't say the exact word so nope.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![abduct](abduct.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> This was a very funny horror comedy/thriller that at times felt even a bit Super Coen Brotherish, with the ways things go off the rails here and there and how people react. I had lots of fun, it is exactly as long as it should be and the "mystery" is surprisingly engaging. Nice!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> I liked this character, Patrick, who was so checked out in an "i'm not even part of this movie, let me be" kind of way that was for once refreshing.
