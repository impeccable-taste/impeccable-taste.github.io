<span class="reviewsection">Premise:</span> So here we have an annoying influencer (but i repeat myself hur hur) that one day decides to pivot from crappy sketches, joke videos and challenges to documenting the alleged haunting of his scottish flat. And this is a documentary with interviews and all that about how did that go for him, if it was all worth it or not. It's a horror movie, you get exactly one guess!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">Under 90 minutes?</span> Just under that!<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Do they say the title?</span> Nah, geez.

<span class="reviewsection">First thing I thought of:</span>

![death-of-a-vlogger](death-of-a-vlogger.gif)<br/>
<span class="reviewsection">Okay, more:</span> Well this was a fun one. I kept thinking "I *know* these people" and yeah, it's the same team that filmed [***Hostile Dimensions***]({% post_url 2025/2025-05-20/2025-05-20-00-hostile-dimensions %}) and just like that one, this is a pretty fun movie. I think it does a better job with the ending than that other one, it is also sometimes very funny (specially the extremely punchable ghost hunter guy) and it manages to be pretty creepy and tense even though it also must have had the budget of a very very small theatre classroom. Nice!<br/>

<span class="reviewsection">A simple way to improve it:</span> So there's this 360 scene that the documentary tells us has been "flattened" but (supposedly) you could turn things around and all that when it was "first shown". Now the thing is, *something* happens there and we are only shown the outcome... and i was like "just... just turn the camera a little to the left there!" but anyway, that's just nitpicking. It kinda works better if you don't see what happened after all.
